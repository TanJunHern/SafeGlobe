Section: Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) & State Connections
Clause 3.6

Any counterparty where a director, senior executive, beneficial owner (>10%), or individual subject is declared as or matched to a Politically Exposed Person (PEP) requires immediate triggering of Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) Questionnaire and source of funds declaration.
