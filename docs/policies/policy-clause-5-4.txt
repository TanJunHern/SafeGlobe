Section: Entity-Specific Risk Controls
Clause 5.4 (Maritime & Vessels)

Maritime vessels flying flags of convenience (e.g., Panama, Liberia, Marshall Islands) or exhibiting recorded AIS telemetry dark periods exceeding 12 hours require mandatory vessel questionnaire submission, charterer verification, and IMO registry audit.
