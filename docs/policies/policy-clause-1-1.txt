Demo policy: written for the DueDilly demo. The company and its policy are fictional.

Section: General Principles
Clause 1.1 (Sanctions screening, demo clause)

Every counterparty, its directors, shareholders and beneficial owners, and every vessel by IMO number, must be screened against the sanctions lists before onboarding and at each re-screen. A name-only match with strong similarity is a possible match and must be investigated. A match confirmed by a second identifier (date of birth, registration number or IMO number) must be escalated, and the relationship must not proceed.
