What this shows
  • Boosted trees beats the rules on every measure: recall up from 36.4% to 63.6%, and 12 of the 28 cases the rules missed are now flagged for a second look.
  • Gemini also improves on the rules for recall and ROC AUC with no training at all, and gets better with examples. It is 95% consistent across repeat runs and scored all 800 reviews (800/800 coverage, 0 failed calls).
  • A single live Gemini call takes 1.18 s median (1.54 s p95, 20 of 20 succeeded).
  • Up arrows mark an improvement over the rules engine alone.
Test setup: local server, Apple M1 (8 cores, 8 GB), Node v24.13.1, SQLite, demo workspace data. Gemini via AI Studio (gemini-flash-lite-latest, resolved to gemini-3.5-flash-lite), temperature 0. Model results describe synthetic demo data, not accuracy on real cases.
