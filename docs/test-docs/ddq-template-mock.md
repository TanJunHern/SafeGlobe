# Maritime Counterparty DDQ (Short Form) Template

## Purpose and instructions

[Issuing Company] asks every vessel owner, operator, charterer and maritime service provider to complete this short questionnaire before a contract is signed. The answers are used to assess bribery, sanctions and vessel-conduct risk.

**How to complete it**

1. Answer every question. Write "Not applicable" with a reason rather than leaving a field blank.
2. Where you answer Yes, give full details in the space provided.
3. Answers cover the company, its parent, its subsidiaries and every vessel it owns, operates or manages.
4. Return the signed questionnaire with the supporting documents to [compliance@issuingcompany.com] within [14] days.

**Supporting documents to attach**

- [ ] Certificate of incorporation or business registration extract (issued within the last 3 months)
- [ ] Shareholder register and ownership chart down to the Beneficial Owners
- [ ] Certificate of registry and P&I certificate of entry for each vessel
- [ ] Copies of the policies referred to in Section 6

## Declaration

This declaration must be signed by a director or an officer authorised to bind the company.

1. I am authorised to complete this questionnaire and sign this declaration on the company's behalf.
2. The information given is true, complete and accurate to the best of my knowledge after making reasonable enquiries.
3. The company will notify [Issuing Company] in writing within [30] days of any change that makes an answer inaccurate.
4. The company consents to [Issuing Company] verifying these answers and screening the company, its key persons and its vessels on an ongoing basis.
5. The company understands that a false or incomplete answer may lead to rejection or termination of any contract.

## Appendix: Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Adverse Media | Negative news or public reports linking a person, company or vessel to financial crime, corruption, Sanctions breaches or serious misconduct. |
| Beneficial Owner | An individual who ultimately owns or controls 25% or more of the company, directly or through other entities, or who otherwise controls its decisions. |
| Government Official | Any officer or employee of a government, ministry, agency or state-owned enterprise, and any political party official or candidate. |
| Politically Exposed Person (PEP) | An individual who holds or has held a prominent public function, and their family members and close associates. |
| Sanctioned Jurisdiction | A country or territory subject to comprehensive Sanctions by the UN, the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom or Singapore. |
| Sanctioned Person | A person, company or vessel named on a Sanctions list, or owned 50% or more by one or more such persons. |
